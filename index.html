About The Book

"The Last Card" by Prakhar Awasthi is a story set across the diverse landscapes of India, introducing three characters—Veer, Vikram, and Sen. Their lives intertwine through a game of cards, revealing tales of love, betrayal, and sacrifice. The narrative explores how their individual stories converge, leading to a shared and poignant ending

Title Page

Every wound, no matter how painful or deep, eventually leaves behind a scar. These scars, though often seen as imperfections, hold stories within them—stories of struggles endured, battles fought, and resilience found. Each mark on the skin is a testament to the strength it took to overcome the hardships that created it. They are reminders of the moments we thought we wouldn’t make it but somehow did. Every scar whispers a tale of survival, courage, and the indomitable spirit of moving forward, no matter how tough the journey. It says, "I lived through this. I triumphed. I survived."
